Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Oak Leaf Tree Service ("we," "us") collects and uses information when you visit oakleaftreeservice.com (the "Site").

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a form on this Site (for example, to request an estimate), we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, property address, and details about the service you're requesting. If you choose to upload a photo, that photo is also sent to us as part of your request.

2. Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, this Site uses privacy-focused analytics to understand general traffic patterns (such as how many people visit and which pages are popular). This analytics service does not use cookies and does not track you individually across other websites.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry or estimate request
  • To schedule and provide the services you request
  • To communicate with you about your service

We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How Your Information Is Sent to Us

Form submissions on this Site are processed through a third-party service that delivers your message directly to our email inbox. That service does not use your information for any purpose beyond delivering your submission to us.

5. Data Retention

We retain the information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and provide our services, or as required by law.

6. Your Choices

You can always contact us directly by phone instead of using the form if you'd prefer not to submit your information online.

7. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to info@oakleaftreeservice.com or (555) 123-4567.
