FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does tree removal cost?

The cost of tree removal varies based on the tree's size, location, and condition. We offer free, no-obligation estimates to give you an exact price before any work begins.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes! All initial consultations and estimates are 100% free. Just give us a call or fill out our contact form.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. We are fully licensed and carry comprehensive liability insurance and workers' compensation. We can provide documentation upon request.

Do you provide emergency tree service?

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency tree removal for storm damage and hazardous situations. Call our emergency line any time, day or night.

Do you clean up after the job?

Always. We pride ourselves on leaving your property cleaner than we found it. All branches and debris are hauled away unless you request to keep the woodchips.

How quickly can you schedule a job?

For non-emergency work, we can usually schedule within 1-2 weeks. Emergency calls are prioritized for same-day or next-day service.
